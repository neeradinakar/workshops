01THE OPPORTUNITY

A COMPLEX PROCESS. A HUMAN NEED.

The most important lines on a map can be the hardest to find.

Land surveying is often arranged through scattered referrals and phone calls. Customers need clarity; surveyors need a dependable way to be discovered and booked. Squared explores a calmer, more transparent path from the first search to the final report.
